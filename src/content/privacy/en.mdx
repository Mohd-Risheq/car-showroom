This site counts visits without cookies and without personal data. This page lists everything it keeps: what, why, for how long and where.

## What the site counts

When you open a page or use something on it, your browser can send the site a short message: what happened, the car it was about, if any, and the page's language. The site adds the message to that day's total, by the date in Amman, and keeps only the total. Nothing about you is kept with it: no address, no browser details, no cookie and no text.

A message names one of these, and the site drops any other:

- opening the home page, the list of cars or a car's page
- using a car page's features: the body scan, the engine sound, the 360° view, the journey and the cost to run
- running the drag race on the compare page, counted for the whole site rather than for a car
- tapping WhatsApp, Call or Request a viewing
- sharing a car, saving a car to My Garage or adding one to compare
- the dealer side: publishing a car, and making a share kit or a reel, each counted for the whole site rather than for a car
- an error in your browser: the day's count goes to the database, and the kind of page and a short code go to the server's log, never the page's address

A feature counts once in each page you open, however often you use it, and a contact, a share, a save or a comparison counts each time. A car's messages count only while the site shows that car. Messages from search engines, link previews and other programs that say they're automated aren't counted, and neither is anything past 60 messages a minute from one network.

If your browser sends Global Privacy Control, the site's pages send no messages, and the site ignores any message that carries the signal.

## Cookies

For a visitor, the only cookie the site sets is NEXT_LOCALE, and only when you choose a language. On the dealer side, signing in sets \_\_Secure-better-auth.session_token, and a sign-in made not to be remembered also sets \_\_Secure-better-auth.dont_remember, which says so, until the browser closes. Each is needed for what it does, so there's no cookie banner. Counting visits uses no cookies.

## Where it's kept

The site runs on Vercel, whose servers in Frankfurt run its code. Pages reach you through Vercel's network from a server near you, and like any web host, Vercel receives your network address to send them. The database is Neon's, on Amazon's servers in Frankfurt. Frankfurt is in Germany, outside Jordan.

The pages load no trackers and no scripts from other sites.

## What you can do

The site keeps no name, contact details or network address of any visitor. What your browser keeps is yours to delete at any time, in its settings, and with Global Privacy Control on, your visits aren't counted.
